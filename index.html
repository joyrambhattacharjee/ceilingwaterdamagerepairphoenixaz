Diagnosis First

Finding the Source, Not Just the Symptom

Water rarely enters directly above the stain. It travels along the top of the ceiling and down the framing — a "chimney effect" of moisture migration — then drips through at the lowest point, often feet from where it got in. Treating the stain without finding the source guarantees it comes back.

Common culprits we trace in Phoenix:

  • Plumbing leaks — supply lines, drains and fittings above the ceiling
  • Roof leaks — worn flashing, cracked tiles and monsoon-driven rain
  • HVAC & condensate — clogged AC drain lines from attic air handlers
  • Water heaters — tank and fitting failures, especially attic or closet units

We confirm the source with infrared leak detection and moisture meters, so the repair addresses the real problem instead of hiding it.

Assessing the Damage

Understanding the Scope: Water Categories & Structure

Not all water damage is equal. We assess contamination, structure and material before we plan the repair.

Water Categories 1–3

Clean supply water (Category 1), gray water with contaminants (Category 2), and grossly contaminated water such as sewage or certain storm water (Category 3). The category sets the safe cleaning and sanitizing required.

Structural Evaluation

We check beyond the drywall — inspecting joists and support beams for saturation, because prolonged moisture can weaken the framing that holds your ceiling up.

Material-Specific Impact

Drywall and plaster respond differently to water. We tailor the approach to your ceiling, whether it is modern drywall or older plaster over lath.

Special Care

Older Phoenix & Historic Homes

Central-Phoenix and historic neighborhoods have ceilings that need a gentler, more informed hand. Plaster-over-lath ceilings crack and stain differently than modern drywall and call for specialized repair that preserves the home's character.

Older textured ceilings carry an important caution: finishes installed before the 1980s can contain asbestos, and older paint can contain lead — both only hazardous when disturbed. We never aggressively scrape or sand an older ceiling without first advising you on testing, keeping your household safe.

The desert is dry most of the year — then monsoon season arrives. That swing is hard on roofs, plumbing and attics across the Valley.

AC Condensate Leaks

Aging Plumbing
