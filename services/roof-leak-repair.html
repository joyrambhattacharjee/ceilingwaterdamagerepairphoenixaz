The Service

From the Roof Line to Your Ceiling

A roof leak almost never drips straight down. Water travels along the top of the ceiling, follows framing and shows up on the drywall feet away from where it actually entered. That's why we trace the moisture back before assuming where the problem is.

We specialize in the interior side of roof leaks — the wet attic insulation, the soaked ceiling framing and the stained, sagging drywall below. For the roof itself, we identify the likely entry point and coordinate the repair so your freshly restored ceiling stays dry.

Common Phoenix Causes

Why Roofs Leak Into Ceilings

Monsoon Storms

Wind-driven summer rain forces water under tiles and through worn flashing.

Aging Flashing

Cracked or lifted flashing around vents and valleys lets water in at the seams.

Flat-Roof Pooling

Low-slope and flat roofs common in the Valley can pond water and seep through.

Cracked Tiles

Broken or slipped roof tiles expose the underlayment to the elements.

Clogged Drainage

Debris-filled scuppers and valleys back water up under roofing materials.

Sun-Baked Materials

Years of intense UV dry out and crack roofing, caulk and sealants.
