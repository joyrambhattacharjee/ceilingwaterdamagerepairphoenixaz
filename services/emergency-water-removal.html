The Service

When Every Minute Counts

Water damage gets worse the longer it sits. In the first hour, water spreads into flooring and walls; within a day, drywall swells and mold risk climbs. Fast extraction is the single biggest factor in limiting how much of your home is affected — and how big the repair becomes.

When you call our emergency line, we help you make the situation safe over the phone, then dispatch a crew to extract the water and set up drying. The sooner we start, the less there is to rebuild.

While You Wait

What to Do Before We Arrive

🚨

Stay Safe

Keep away from sagging ceilings and avoid water near electrical fixtures and outlets.

🚽

Stop the Source

If you can do so safely, shut off the water supply or the main valve.

📦

Move Valuables

Relocate furniture, electronics and keepsakes away from the wet area.

💧

Contain Drips

Place a bucket under active drips and lay down towels to limit spread.

📷

Photograph It

Take photos and video for your records and your insurance claim.

📞

Call Us

Reach our 24/7 line so we can guide you and dispatch a crew.
