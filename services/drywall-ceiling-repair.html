The Service

Finishing Work That Actually Blends In

The difference between an obvious patch and an invisible repair is in the finishing. Hanging a new piece of drywall is the easy part — feathering the joint compound, re-creating the exact texture and blending the paint is where experience shows.

We repair everything from a single water-stained ceiling section to an entire room's ceiling after a major leak, including the baseboards, trim and wall drywall that water often damages on its way down.

The Finishing Steps

How We Rebuild a Ceiling

Cut & Remove

We score and remove damaged sheetrock back to sound material and clean framing.

Re-hang & Fasten

New board is cut to fit and fastened to the ceiling joists or furring.

Tape & Mud

Seams are taped and coated, then sanded flat with dust containment in place.

Texture Match

We spray or hand-apply texture to match popcorn, knockdown or orange-peel.

Prime & Paint

Stain-blocking primer seals old marks; finish paint blends the repair.

Detail & Clean

Trim, baseboards and the work area are finished and cleaned up.

Beyond the Ceiling

Texture & Paint Matching

Arizona homes feature a wide range of ceiling textures, and each one needs a different technique to reproduce. We identify your texture, test our match on a hidden area when needed, and adjust until it disappears into the surrounding ceiling.

  • Knockdown and skip-trowel textures
  • Spray and hand-applied popcorn
  • Orange-peel and splatter finishes
  • Smooth, level-5 ceilings
