The Science of Drying a Home

Effective drying is about psychrometrics — the balance of temperature, humidity and airflow. We set up the right number of air movers and dehumidifiers for the space, then take daily moisture readings to confirm materials are actually drying, not just feeling dry.

Phoenix's low outdoor humidity helps, but water hides in places air can't reach: inside wall cavities, under flooring and above ceilings. Thermal imaging and moisture meters tell us where that hidden water is so nothing gets left wet behind a finished surface.

Why Monitoring Matters

We Dry to a Number, Not a Guess

"Dry to the touch" is not dry. Drywall and framing can hold moisture well above safe levels while feeling fine on the surface. We set target moisture contents based on unaffected materials in your home and keep equipment running until the readings get there.

  • Target moisture content set from dry reference points
  • Daily readings until targets are met
  • Equipment adjusted as the structure dries
  • Documentation handed to you at completion
